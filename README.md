### 🌌 About Me

I am a software engineer and systems architect focused on **high-performance computing**, **physics simulations**, **interactive 2D/3D graphics engines**, and **native desktop & mobile desktop applications**.

- 🔭 **Latest Flagship**: [AeroPro: Rocketry & Propulsion Studio](https://github.com/valliente/aeropro-rocketry-studio) — 1976 US Standard Atmosphere + RK4 ascent trajectory + supersonic nozzle engine.
- ⚡ **Core Focus**: Rust, C++20, TypeScript, Python, Tauri v2, Capacitor, WebAudio DSP, and Qt6.
- 💬 **Collaborations**: Open to aerospace physics engines, real-time audio DSP, and high-performance simulation tooling.
